MotoGrid Rider Tiers

Race. Improve. Rank Up.

MotoGrid uses a 24-tier MMR ladder to track rider progression across verified race results. Every division spans 1,000 MMR, beginning at Copper I and climbing all the way to Magenta III.

Total Divisions 24 Tiers
Division Size 1,000 MMR
Top Division Magenta III
01 // EARN

Earn MMR

Upload a valid race result with at least 12 classified riders. Your verified finish determines the MMR earned.

02 // PROGRESS

Climb Divisions

Each 1,000 MMR milestone advances you into a new division and unlocks a more advanced badge design.

03 // COMPETE

Own Your Rank

Your tier appears alongside your MotoGrid standings position so other riders can see exactly where you sit.

The 24 Tiers

The badge evolves as the rider climbs. Higher divisions add more aggressive framing, laurels, stars, crystals and championship-style details while keeping the Roman numeral at the center.
